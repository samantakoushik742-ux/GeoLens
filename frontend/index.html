ABOUT GEOLENS

Reading environmental change from orbit, one place at a time.

GeoLens is an environmental monitoring and disaster-prediction platform for West Bengal. It follows three kinds of change (flooding, drought and vegetation loss) across places that are hit again and again, from the Sundarbans delta to the Darjeeling hills.

Instead of looking at a single satellite photo, GeoLens compares an older image and a recent image of the same place, and asks a trained model what changed and how serious it looks.

02How the analysis works

  1. Step 1

    Two images, one place

    A historical and a recent satellite image (GeoTIFF) of the same area are paired together.

  2. Step 2

    Clean & align

    Invalid pixels are zeroed, each band is clipped to its 2nd–98th percentile, both images are normalised with shared statistics so real change isn't erased, then resized to 256×256.

  3. Step 3

    Temporal CNN

    One shared convolutional encoder turns each image into 512 features. The model looks at the old features, the new features and the difference between them (1,536 features in total).

  4. Step 4

    Class & risk

    It predicts normal, flood, wildfire, cyclone or landslide, then combines confidence and a change score into a LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk level.

Please note

The risk level is a project-level estimate based on model confidence and how much the image changed. It is not a certified emergency warning. For alerts, follow IMD and the West Bengal disaster management department.
